# Voice Call

Voice call lets you talk to AI like a phone call — no typing needed, just speak and AI responds.

---

## Starting a Voice Call

1. In the chat page, tap the **🎤 Voice Call** button above the input box
2. Wait for the connection to establish (shows "Calling...")
3. Once connected, the status changes to "In Call"
4. Speak into your microphone — AI hears you and responds

---

## In-Call Controls

| Action | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Mute | Tap mute — AI won't hear you |
| Unmute | Tap again to unmute |
| Speaker | Toggle speaker playback |
| Hang Up | Tap the red hang-up button to end the call |

---

## Call Features

- **Real-time conversation**: AI responds almost instantly after you speak
- **Speech recognition**: Your words are automatically transcribed and shown in chat history
- **Speech synthesis**: AI's replies are read aloud in natural voice
- **Character sync**: The character model lip-syncs and makes expressions during the call
- **Memory saved**: Call content is saved to chat history and long-term memory

---

## Call Records

After the call ends, a call record card appears in chat history, including:

- Call type (Voice Call)
- Call duration
- Conversation transcript from the call

You can review past call content at any time.

---

## Common Issues

### No sound during voice call?
- Check that device volume is turned up
- Check that microphone permission has been granted
- Try plugging/unplugging headphones or toggling speaker mode

### AI can't hear me?
- Confirm microphone permission is enabled
- Check if you accidentally tapped the mute button
- Try speaking closer to the microphone
