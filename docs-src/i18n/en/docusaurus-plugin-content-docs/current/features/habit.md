# Habit Tracking

Habit tracking helps you maintain and track daily habits. Whether it's drinking water, exercising, reading, or meditating, AI is there with you every step of the way.

---

## Creating Habits

### Via Conversation

Tell AI what habit you want to build:

- "Help me create a habit of drinking 8 glasses of water daily"
- "I want to run 3 times a week"
- "Track my daily 15-minute meditation habit"

AI creates a habit card with name, frequency, and target count.

### Habit Parameters

| Parameter | Description | Options |
|-----------|-------------|---------|
| Name | Habit name | Custom |
| Frequency | Check-in cycle | Daily / Weekly / Monthly |
| Target Count | Completions per cycle | Number |

---

## Check-In

### Daily Check-In

- On the **Widgets** page habit card, tap the + button to increment today's count
- Or tell AI: "I drank 3 glasses of water today"
- When the target count is reached, the habit shows as completed ✓

### View Progress

The habit card shows:

- Today's count / target count
- Progress bar
- Completion status (✓ Done / ○ Not yet)

---

## Habit Management

### View All Habits

- **Widgets** page: Habit tracking widget
- **Life** page: Full habit tracking view

### Delete a Habit

Tell AI: "Stop tracking the water drinking habit", or delete it from the habit list.

---

## AI & Habits

AI actively participates in your habit-building journey:

- **Create**: Automatically creates habits when habit needs are detected in conversation
- **Check-in**: Records completions when you tell AI you've done something
- **Encourage**: Gives encouragement when you complete habits
- **Summarize**: Reports habit completion in daily summaries

---

## Tips

- Start with small habits and easy-to-reach targets
- Leverage AI's companionship to make persistence less lonely
- Share habit completions with AI daily for positive feedback
