# To-Do Management

Eidolon's to-do management lets you and AI manage daily tasks together. Just tell AI what you need to do, and it creates a to-do card with priority and due date.

---

## Creating To-Dos

### Via Conversation

The easiest way — just tell AI:

- "Remind me to submit the report tomorrow"
- "Add moving this weekend to my to-do list"
- "Create a to-do: meeting next Monday at 10 AM, urgent and important"

AI automatically creates a to-do card with title, priority, and due date.

### Via Home Widget

On the **"Today"** home page or **Widgets** page to-do card:

1. Tap the input box at the bottom of the to-do list
2. Enter the to-do title
3. Press Enter to add

---

## To-Do Information

Each to-do contains:

| Field | Description | Example |
|-------|-------------|---------|
| Title | Task name | Submit report |
| Note | Supplementary description | Must include Q3 data |
| Priority | Four-quadrant classification | Urgent & Important |
| Due Date | Deadline | 2024-12-20 |

### Priority Guide

| Priority | Meaning | Suggestion |
|----------|---------|------------|
| Urgent & Important | Must handle immediately | Do first |
| Important Not Urgent | Important but can wait | Schedule time |
| Urgent Not Important | Needs immediate action but low impact | Consider delegating |
| Not Urgent Not Important | Neither urgent nor important | Consider dropping |

---

## Managing To-Dos

### Complete a To-Do

- Check the box on the left of the to-do in the list
- Or tell AI: "Mark the report submission as done"

### Delete a To-Do

- Tell AI: "Delete that expired to-do"
- Or delete it from the to-do list

### View To-Dos

- **Today** home: Shows active to-dos
- **Widgets** page: Full to-do list
- **Life** page: View in the life planner

---

## AI & To-Dos

AI proactively helps manage your to-dos:

- **Create**: Automatically creates to-dos when task needs are detected in conversation
- **Remind**: Reminds you when due dates are approaching
- **Complete**: Marks tasks done when you tell AI you've finished something
- **Summarize**: Reports to-do completion status in daily summaries

---

## Tips

- Make a habit of telling AI your plans — it helps you manage better
- Use the priority quadrants to focus on what matters
- Regularly review your to-do list and clear completed/expired tasks
