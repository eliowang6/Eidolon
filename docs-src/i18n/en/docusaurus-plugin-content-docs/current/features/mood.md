# Mood Tracking

Mood tracking lets you record how you feel at any moment. AI pays attention to your emotional changes and offers companionship and support when needed.

---

## Recording Your Mood

### Quick Record

In the **"Today"** home page mood section:

1. Select your current mood:
   - 😊 **Good** — Feeling great
   - 😐 **Okay** — Nothing special
   - 😟 **Not Great** — Feeling down

2. Choose a reason (optional):
   - 💼 Work / Study
   - 👥 Relationships
   - 🎮 Games / Entertainment
   - 🏃 Physical State
   - 💭 Inner State
   - 🏠 Daily Life

3. Add a note (optional):
   - Write whatever comes to mind about this moment

4. Save

### Via Conversation

You can also just tell AI how you feel:

- "I'm not feeling great today, work stress is really getting to me"
- "So happy! I passed the exam!"
- "Feeling just okay, nothing special"

AI understands your emotions and responds accordingly.

---

## Mood Journey

### Mood Trajectory Chart

On the **"Us"** page and **Journey** page, you can see:

- Mood change curve over the last 48 hours
- Mood markers for each recording
- Distribution of good/low/neutral moods

### Trajectory Summary

The system generates summaries based on your mood records:

- "Life seems quite colorful over the past two days"
- "Good mood has been your constant companion"
- "Seems like you're feeling some frustration or setbacks"

---

## Value of Mood Tracking

- **Self-awareness**: Understand your emotional patterns better through recording
- **AI companionship**: AI adjusts its communication style based on your mood
- **Emotional review**: Look back at mood changes over time and discover patterns
- **Daily summaries**: Mood data feeds into AI-generated daily summaries

---

## Privacy

Mood records are your private data, visible only to you and AI. AI will not disclose your emotional information to other users.

---

## Tips

- You don't need to write a lot each time — a single emoji is enough
- Record low moods too — it helps AI understand you better
- Regularly review your mood trajectory to stay mindful of your mental health
