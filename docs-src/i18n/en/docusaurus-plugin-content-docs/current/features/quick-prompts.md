# Quick Prompts

Quick Prompts are pre-made conversation templates — start a common-scenario chat with one click, no need to think about how to begin.

---

## Using Quick Prompts

1. Find the **"Quick Prompts"** area above the chat input box
2. Tap any quick prompt button
3. AI automatically initiates the corresponding conversation

---

## Available Quick Prompts

| Quick Prompt | Description | Example Dialogue |
|--------------|-------------|------------------|
| 🎭 **Mini Drama** | Enter interactive story mode | Start an immersive story |
| 🍴 **What to Eat** | Get food recommendations | "What do you think I should eat?" |
| 🗺️ **Where to Go** | Get outing suggestions | "Help me think of somewhere to go" |
| 📝 **Solve Question** | Take a photo for AI to solve | "Teach me how to solve this" (requires photo) |
| 💬 **Q&A Game** | Interactive Q&A game | "Let's play Q&A" |
| 🔍 **Analyze This** | Take a photo for AI to analyze | "Help me analyze what's in this picture" (requires photo) |
| 🧩 **Brain Teaser** | Play brain teasers | "Let's play brain teasers" |

---

## Photo-Based Quick Prompts

**Solve Question** and **Analyze This** require taking a photo:

1. After tapping the quick prompt, the camera opens automatically
2. Aim at what you want to capture and take a photo
3. The photo is automatically sent to AI
4. AI analyzes the photo content and responds

---

## Tips

- When you don't know what to talk about, try a quick prompt
- "What to Eat" and "Where to Go" give recommendations based on your preferences
- "Q&A Game" and "Brain Teaser" are great for casual relaxation
- "Solve Question" is a great study companion
