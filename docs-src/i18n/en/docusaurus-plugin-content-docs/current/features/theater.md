# Theater

Theater is Eidolon's immersive interactive storytelling feature. Create or enter a story world and co-create a unique adventure with AI.

---

## What Is Theater?

Theater is an **interactive narrative** system:

- You are the protagonist, AI is the narrator and world builder
- Every choice you make affects the story's direction
- AI generates unique plots based on your settings
- Stories can include combat, puzzles, emotions, exploration, and more

---

## Creating a New Story

1. Click **"Theater"** in the navigation bar
2. Click **"Create New Story"**
3. Choose **Manual Setup** or **Random Generate**

### Random Generate

Click random generate, and the system automatically creates a complete story setting including world, characters, and plot direction. Great for quick starts.

### Manual Setup

Fine-tune every detail of your story:

#### World Settings

| Setting | Description | Example |
|---------|-------------|---------|
| Primary Genre | Main story type | Fantasy, Sci-Fi, Urban, Historical |
| Sub Genre | Secondary type | Mystery, Romance, Adventure |
| World Framework | Underlying world rules | Magic world, Cyberpunk, Real world |
| World Rules | Special world laws | Magic has a cost, AI has consciousness |
| Tone | Story atmosphere | Dark, Lighthearted, Epic |
| Pace | Story progression speed | Tight, Relaxed, Balanced |

#### Character Settings

| Setting | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Protagonist Name | Your name in the story |
| Protagonist Identity | Your role |
| Protagonist Goal | What you want to achieve |
| Protagonist Secret | Something you're hiding |
| Protagonist Weakness | Your vulnerability |
| AI Character Name | Name of the AI character |
| AI Character Identity | Role of the AI character |
| AI Character Relationship | Relationship between you and AI |

#### Plot Settings

| Setting | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Plot Focus | World / Mechanics / Relationship / Balanced |
| Plot Direction | Mystery / Struggle / Emotion / Growth |
| Task Mode | None / Soft / Explicit |
| System Style | None / Gentle / Teasing / Sardonic / Strict |
| Opening Situation | Scene when the story begins |

## Playing the Story

After creating or selecting a story, enter the story dialogue:

- You input actions or dialogue
- AI describes scene changes and NPC reactions
- The story progresses continuously

### Action Options

AI sometimes presents action options:

| Type | Marker | Meaning |
|------|--------|---------|
| Positive | Green | A choice that moves things forward |
| Unexpected | Blue | An unforeseen development |
| Negative | Red | May bring trouble |
| Skip | Gray | Skip the current scene |
| Charged | Gold | Build up power |

You can also ignore preset options and freely type any action.

---

## Story Management

### Story List

The Theater page shows all your stories:

- Sorted by recent activity
- Shows title, status, and last message time
- Click to continue the story

### Branch Stories

Create a branch from an existing story:

- Select **"Create Branch"** from the story menu
- Enter a title for the new branch
- The branch inherits the original settings but develops independently

### Edit Story

Modify story settings at any time — adjust the world, characters, and plot parameters.

---

## Story Images

During the story, AI may generate scene images based on the plot for enhanced immersion.

---

## Tips

- Start with random generation to quickly experience Theater's charm
- Try different genre combinations for unexpected stories
- Free-form actions are more fun than picking preset options
- Co-create a unique story with AI that belongs to just the two of you
