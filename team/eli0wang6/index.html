RESEARCH INTERESTS

What I work on

Four threads that run through my research — from model efficiency to the long-term relationship between people and the systems they build.

01 / EFFICIENCY

Efficient Large Language Models

Exploring methods to improve intelligence density, adaptive computation, and model efficiency — getting more capability out of fewer parameters and less compute.

Intelligence Density Adaptive Compute Sparse Architectures
02 / AGENCY

Autonomous AI Agents

Building AI systems with persistent memory, planning capability, and long-term interaction — agents that act, remember, and stay accountable over time.

Persistent Memory Planning Tool Use
03 / SYMBIOSIS

Human–AI Symbiosis

Investigating new forms of collaboration between humans and digital intelligence — how trust, presence, and shared context develop across a long-lived relationship.

Collaboration Trust Presence
04 / PERSONALIZATION

Personalized AI Systems

Developing architectures for long-term, context-aware AI companions that accumulate understanding of an individual rather than resetting every session.

Long-term Context Continuity Companion Systems

Background

Elio Wang is an independent AI researcher and founder of Instarmony AI Lab.
