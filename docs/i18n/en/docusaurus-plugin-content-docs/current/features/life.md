# Life Feed

**Life Feed** (Life) is Sholarel's all-in-one life dashboard. It brings your to-dos, habit tracking, mood logs and AI summaries together, so you can see your day at a glance.

---

## Opening the Life Feed

Click **"Life"** in the navigation bar to open the Life Feed page.

---

## What's on the Page

The page has several views you can switch between from the top:

### Timeline View

Shows your daily activity as a timeline:

- Daily and instant summaries generated by AI
- Mood logs and emotional shifts
- To-do completion
- Habit check-in progress
- Notable conversation snippets

### List View

Collects everything in one list:

- All to-dos (sorted by priority and due date)
- All habit check-ins and today's progress
- Mood log history

### Insights View

Analysis AI generates from your activity data:

- Recent mood trends
- Habit completion rate
- To-do completion efficiency
- AI suggestions and encouragement

---

## Daily Summary

At the end of each day, AI automatically writes a **daily summary** covering:

- An overview of today's conversations
- To-do progress
- Habit check-ins
- A review of mood changes
- Personalized suggestions from AI

:::info Subscription requirement
Daily summaries require Presence or a higher subscription tier.
:::

---

## Instant Summary

Besides the daily summary, you can ask AI for an **instant summary** at any time:

- Tell AI in chat: "Summarize my activity today"
- AI analyzes the day's data and writes a combined report

---

## Widgets Page

The **Widgets** page offers standalone widget views for a quick look at key information:

| Widget | Shows |
|--------|-------|
| Today's Summary | AI's overview of the day |
| To-Do List | To-dos still in progress |
| Habit Check-ins | Today's habit progress |
| Mood Log | Your current mood and recent entries |

### Desktop Widgets (Desktop)

On desktop, widgets can stay pinned to your desktop as **floating windows**:

1. Go to **Settings → Preferences → Desktop Widgets**
2. Turn on the desktop widgets switch
3. Widgets appear on the desktop as floating windows

This way you can keep an eye on to-do progress and habits while working in other apps.

---

## The "Us" Page

The **"Us"** page shows the memories and relationship data you share with AI:

| Data | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Days together | Days since you met AI |
| Sentences exchanged | Total messages exchanged |
| Sentences today | Messages exchanged today |
| Mood entries | All mood logs so far |
| Emotional track | Recent mood curve |
| Shared topics | Topics you talk about most |
| Achievements | Conversation milestones |

It's a record of the time you've spent with AI, and a window into how deep the relationship has grown.

---

## Tips

- The Life Feed is a good place to review and plan — take a minute each day to look at your summary
- Desktop widgets suit situations where you need to keep track of to-dos and habits continuously
- The "Us" page collects the little moments you share with AI, and gets richer over time
