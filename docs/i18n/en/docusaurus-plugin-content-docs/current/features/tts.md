# TTS Voice Settings

Eidolon's TTS (Text-to-Speech) feature lets AI reply with natural-sounding voice. You can adjust emotional intensity and even upload your own voice sample so AI speaks in your voice.

---

## Voice Emotion Intensity

Adjust how expressive AI's voice sounds:

1. Go to **Settings → TTS**
2. Find the **Emotion Intensity** slider
3. Drag the slider to adjust

| Intensity | Effect |
|-----------|--------|
| Low | Steady, restrained tone |
| Medium | Natural, moderate emotion |
| High | Rich, vivid emotional expression |

---

## Reference Audio

Upload a reference audio clip so AI's voice better matches your desired style.

### Upload Reference Audio

1. Go to **Settings → TTS**
2. Click the **Upload Reference Audio** button
3. Select an audio file (10–30 seconds of clear speech recommended)
4. Wait for upload and processing to complete

### Reference Audio Requirements

- Duration: 10–30 seconds recommended
- Content: Clear human voice recording
- Format: Common audio formats (WAV, MP3, etc.)

### View Current Reference Audio

The settings page shows current reference audio info:

- Whether audio has been uploaded
- File size
- Audio duration
- Upload time

## Voice Cloning (Genesis Exclusive)

<span class="tier-badge tier-genesis">Genesis</span>

Genesis subscribers can use the **Voice Cloning** feature, letting AI speak in your own voice during video calls.

### How to Use

1. Upload a TTS reference audio (see above)
2. The system automatically creates your voice clone
3. In Omni mode video calls, AI speaks in your voice

### Limits

- Maximum **1 upload** per day
- Deleting audio does not reset the daily counter
- Voice cloning only works in Omni video call mode

:::caution Voice Cloning Ethics
Voice cloning is strictly for cloning your own voice. Do not upload other people's voice samples. Abuse of this feature may result in account suspension.
:::

## Common Issues

### AI voice sounds flat?
- Try increasing the emotion intensity slider
- Confirm your subscription tier supports emotional TTS

### Reference audio upload failed?
- Check the audio file format and size
- Make sure the content is clear human speech
- Note the daily upload limit

### Voice clone doesn't sound like me?
- Upload a clearer, longer voice sample
- Record in a quiet environment
- Avoid background noise and other voices
