# FAQ

## Installation & Launch

### What platforms are supported?
Eidolon is available on **Windows Desktop**, **Android**, and **Web**. macOS and iOS versions are planned.

### Windows shows "Windows protected your PC" during installation?
This is Windows SmartScreen warning for unsigned apps. Click "More info" → "Run anyway" to proceed with installation.

### White screen / won't load after launch?
- Check your network connection
- Try disabling antivirus software and restarting
- Confirm your system meets requirements (Windows 10 1803+)

### Android installation failed?
- Confirm system version is Android 8.0+
- Enable "Allow installation from unknown sources"
- Check available storage space

## Account

### Forgot my password?
Currently, please contact the admin to reset your password. Self-service recovery will be available in future versions.

### Can I change my username?
Usernames cannot be changed after registration. Nicknames can be changed anytime.

### Can I log in on multiple devices?
Yes. The same account on different devices automatically syncs data.

---

## Conversations

### AI not responding?
- Check your network connection
- Confirm your "Echo" quota hasn't been used up
- Try refreshing the page or restarting the app

### AI response quality is poor?
- Try expressing your needs more clearly and specifically
- Adjust the system prompt in settings to guide AI's reply style
- Upgrade your subscription for a more powerful AI model

### Will conversation history be lost?
Conversation history is saved in the cloud and auto-syncs after login. Uninstalling and reinstalling doesn't affect cloud data.

---

## Voice & Video

### No sound during voice calls?
- Check device volume
- Confirm microphone and speaker permissions are granted
- Try toggling speaker mode

### Video call connection failed?
- Check network bandwidth (2 Mbps+ recommended)
- Confirm camera permission is granted
- Close other apps using the camera

### Video call auto-hung up?
By default, calls auto-hang up after 60 minutes to prevent quota waste. You can disable this protection in settings.

---

## Subscription & Quota

### What is "Echo" quota?
"Echo" is Eidolon's AI usage quota. Each conversation, image generation, and call consumes a certain amount. Quota refreshes each subscription cycle.

### Quota used up?
- Upgrade your subscription tier
- Wait for quota refresh
- Reduce high-consumption actions

### How to upgrade subscription?
During beta, contact the community admin/creator. The official release will support online payment.

---

## Privacy & Security

### Is my conversation data secure?
- All data uses encrypted transmission
- Conversation content is visible only to you and AI
- Your personal information is not shared with third parties

### Does AI remember everything I say?
AI automatically identifies and remembers important information, but doesn't verbatim record all conversations. You can view and manage what AI remembers on the Memory page.

### Can I delete my data?
Contact the admin to delete your account and associated data.

## Other

### How to report issues?
- Report to the community admin in the group
- Submit an Issue on GitHub
- Each valid report earns 3 days of VIP access

### How to get an invite code?
Join the official community and request one from the admin or content creator.

### What languages are supported?
The interface supports **Simplified Chinese** and **English**, switchable in settings. AI conversations support multiple languages.
